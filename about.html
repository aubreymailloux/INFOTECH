ABOUT AUBREY MAILLOUX

Aubrey Mailloux (she/they) is a movement artist born and raised in Casper, Wyoming.
Mailloux is recent graduate from the Purchase Conservatory of Dance, where they received a BFA
in Dance and a BA in Arts Management summa cum laude and the Abbott Kaplan Award for interdisciplinarity.

In addition to their dance training, Mailloux has pursued hands-on art history experience through
archival internships at Jazz at Lincoln Center, Jacob's Pillow Dance Festival, Purchase Conservatory of Dance,
American Dance Festival, the Neuberger Museum of Art, and the Nicolaysen Art Museum. She is a student of the
Pratt Institute Master of Library and Information Science program.

Mailloux is a maker interested in physicalized and verbalized language as a waypath to explore our place the world.
Mailloux’s work wades through a sea of tenderness and ridiculousness, embracing theatricality and pedestrianism
collaged with dance technique to magnify what makes us. By examining the syntax of the body and speech, Mailloux
wonders how embodied practice reflects the way we carry ourselves. Their work has been presented at Green Space and
DanceAction Garden Series in NYC, Purchase Conservatory of Dance and Forum Art Space, and Wyoming Fringe Festival.
